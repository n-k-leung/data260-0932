Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Purpose. This policy explains how people outside our team can report a security problem in the open
source packages that we publish.

How to report. Send an email to the security contact address listed in the README file of the package.
Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Our response. We confirm that we received a report within 3 business days. We then check the problem and
tell the reporter if we accept it.

Coordinated disclosure. We ask reporters to keep the problem private for 90 days while we prepare a fix.
If the fix is ready earlier, we publish the advisory earlier.

Credit. With the reporter's permission we name them in the published advisory.

Advisories. Every fixed vulnerability gets a security advisory with a CVE number, the affected versions,
and the fixed version.

Scope. This policy covers packages published under our organization on ppm and PyPI. It does not cover
websites, servers or third party packages that we only use.

Safe harbor. We will not start legal action against people who follow this policy, who do not access
private data, and who do not harm our users.

Contact language. Reports may be written in English. We do not offer money rewards for reports at this
time.
